Vibematcher Child Safety Standards

Version 2026-10-17 · Developer: Gopal Agrawal (Vibematcher), Harda, Madhya Pradesh, India

Vibematcher has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to the Vibematcher app and website and to everyone who uses them.

1. Our standards against CSAE

Vibematcher is for adults (18+) only. It is prohibited to use Vibematcher to:

  • create a profile for, or as, anyone under 18;
  • share, request, create or link to CSAM: any visual depiction (photo, video, drawing or computer-generated image) of a minor engaged in sexually explicit conduct;
  • groom a child: building contact or trust with a minor for sexual purposes;
  • sextort, threaten or coerce a child, or traffic a child for sex;
  • sexualise minors in any text, moment, tag or chat;
  • arrange to meet a minor for any sexual or romantic purpose.

Any of these leads to immediate removal and a permanent ban, preservation of evidence, and reporting to the police.

2. How we prevent minors from using Vibematcher

  • Age gate: every user must enter their age and confirm they are 18+ before creating a profile. Entering an age under 18 locks the app on that device.
  • Server check: our server rejects any profile with an age under 18.
  • Google Play: the Android app is listed for an 18+ audience only, with Google Play’s “Restrict Minor Access” setting turned on.
  • Profiles are visible only to people checked in at the same event, never publicly searchable.
  • No location sharing: user locations are never shown to other users.
  • One photo, no nudity, and chat opens only after a mutual match.

3. In-app reporting

Every profile card and every chat has ⋯ → Report, with the option “Underage / child safety”. When someone is reported:

  1. the reported profile is hidden from everyone at once and the reporter and the reported person are blocked from each other;
  2. the report goes to our moderation queue with the recent chat for review;
  3. confirmed CSAE leads to a permanent ban and the steps in section 4.

You can also report directly to our child safety contact (section 6), even if you don’t use the app.

4. How we handle CSAM

When we obtain actual knowledge of CSAM or CSAE on Vibematcher, we:

  • remove or disable access to the content and the account as quickly as possible, and within 2 hours of a complaint about sexual or nude content of an individual (IT Rules 2021, r.3(2)(b));
  • preserve the content and related records, without altering them, for at least 180 days for investigation (IT Rules r.3(1)(g)), or longer if an authority asks;
  • report it to the police / Special Juvenile Police Unit as required by section 19 of the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012, and through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in), and cooperate with law-enforcement requests within the legal timelines (IT Rules r.3(1)(j));
  • never share, forward or re-upload the material except to the authorities as the law requires.

5. Compliance with child safety laws

We comply with applicable child safety laws, including the POCSO Act, 2012, section 67B of the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, the IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (we do not process children’s data), and Google Play’s Child Safety Standards policy.
