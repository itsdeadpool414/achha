Delete your Vibematcher account and data

App: Vibematcher · Developer: Gopal Agrawal (Vibematcher), Harda, Madhya Pradesh, India

You can delete your Vibematcher account and all associated data at any time, free, with no questions asked. Choose the option that suits you.

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Vibematcher and tap Profile (bottom right).
  2. Scroll to Legal & privacy and tap Delete my account & data.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete everything.

Deletion happens immediately on our servers and the app forgets your device ID.

Option 2: On this page (same phone and browser)

If you used Vibematcher in this browser on this phone (or the Android app, which shares Chrome’s storage), you can delete from here without reopening the app.

Option 3: Request by email (any device, or if you uninstalled the app)

Email agrawalgopal777@gmail.com [REPLACE with a dedicated support/grievance email] with the subject “Delete my Vibematcher account” and include as much of this as you can, so we can find the right profile (there is no email or phone number linked to accounts):

  • your Vibematcher ID (Profile → Legal & privacy; 8 characters), if you have it;
  • the first name and age on your profile and your Instagram handle, if you added one;
  • the event(s) you checked in to and roughly when;
  • optionally, a screenshot of your profile.

We acknowledge within 24 hours, confirm the match with you if needed, and complete deletion within 7 days (the legal maximum is 90 days). We will not ask for ID documents.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: first name, age, gender, match preference, photo, vibe tags, Instagram handle
  • Your check-ins, “I felt this” taps and the moments you posted
  • Your likes and passes, all your matches and all messages in those chats (they disappear for the other person too)
  • Your blocks, your consent record and your device secret

Your photo file is removed from storage within 7 days. Your precise location is never stored, so there is nothing to delete.
