Vibematcher Community Guidelines

Version 2026-10-17

Vibematcher is about one thing: the people who felt the same moment as you, at the same event. Keep it real, keep it kind, keep it safe. These rules apply to your profile, photo, moments, chats and how you treat people you meet. Breaking them can get your content removed and your device banned.

Be 18+ and be you

  • 18 or older only. No exceptions. Report anyone who seems younger (“Underage / child safety”).
  • One real photo of yourself. Clear face, no group shots, no celebrities, no photos of other people, no AI-generated or morphed images of a real person.
  • Your real first name and real age. No impersonation, no fake profiles.

Keep it respectful

  • No harassment, bullying, threats, stalking or intimidation.
  • No hate speech or content attacking people for religion, caste, race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, disability or nationality.
  • A pass is a no. Don’t chase people across other apps after they block or ignore you.
  • Consent matters, online and offline. “No”, silence or blocking means stop.

Keep it clean

  • No nudity or sexual content in photos, moments or chats. No sexual requests to someone who hasn’t clearly welcomed them.
  • Nothing sexual involving minors, ever. We remove it, ban the account and report it to the police. See our Child Safety Standards.
  • No sharing intimate images of anyone, real or fake. Victims can report and we act within 2 hours of a complaint.

Keep it honest

  • No scams, spam, promotions, selling, MLM or links to paid services.
  • No paid dating, escort services or “sugar” arrangements (money or gifts in exchange for company or sex).
  • No fake GPS, bots, multiple profiles or scraping.

Protect privacy

  • Don’t share anyone’s phone number, home address, live location, photos or chats without their permission.
  • Moments are public to everyone at the event. Don’t post people’s names or personal details in them.

Meeting up safely

  • Meet at the venue or another busy public place first. Tell a friend.
  • Arrange your own ride home. Keep your drink with you.
  • Don’t share your home address, live location or money details early.
  • Something feels off? Leave, then Block + Report. In an emergency call 112.

Reporting and what happens next

  • Tap ⋯ → Report on any card or chat. The reported profile is hidden from everyone immediately while we review, and you are blocked from each other.
  • We review reports and may dismiss, warn, remove content or ban the device. Serious cases (threats, sexual content involving minors, non-consensual images) are preserved and reported to the authorities.
  • Disagree with a decision, or want to complain? Contact our Grievance Officer.

Enforcement

Depending on how serious it is: content removal, profile hidden, permanent device ban, and reporting to law enforcement. We may act without warning. Repeated or severe violations always mean a ban.
