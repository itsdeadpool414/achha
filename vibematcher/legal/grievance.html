How to complain

  • In the app: tap ⋯ → Report on any profile or chat. This hides the profile immediately while we review.
  • By email to the Grievance Officer. Please include: your Vibematcher ID (Profile → Legal & privacy), what happened, the profile or content concerned (first name, event, time, a screenshot if possible), and what you want us to do. You can complain on someone else’s behalf (for example, a victim who cannot write themselves).

Our timelines

Type of complaint What we do Time limit
Any complaint Acknowledge receipt Within 24 hours
Any complaint Resolve and tell you the outcome Within 7 days (IT Rules r.3(2)(a)(i), as amended 10 Feb 2026)
Request to remove content that breaks our rules under IT Rules r.3(1)(b) (for example obscene, harassing, impersonating or unlawful content) Act on it As fast as possible, within 36 hours
Content that shows your private area, full or partial nudity, or you in a sexual act, or impersonates you (including morphed or AI-generated images) Remove or disable access Within 2 hours of your complaint (IT Rules r.3(2)(b))
Requests about your personal data (access, correction, erasure, nomination, withdrawal of consent) Respond and act Within 7 days where possible, never more than 90 days (DPDP Rules r.14(3))
Court orders or lawful government directions Comply Within the time the IT Rules set (currently 3 hours for takedown orders under r.3(1)(d))

If you are not satisfied

  • Content and account decisions: you can appeal to the Grievance Appellate Committee at gac.gov.in within 30 days of our decision, or if we did not resolve your complaint in time (IT Rules r.3A).
  • Personal data complaints: after using our grievance process, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India under the DPDP Act, 2023.
  • Crimes: report cybercrime at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930; in an emergency call 112.
