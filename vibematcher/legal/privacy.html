Vibematcher Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-17 · Effective 17 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vibematcher (the app and website, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, shares, keeps and deletes your personal data, and how you can use your rights. It is written to meet India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) and Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (“DPDP Rules”), the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 (“IT Rules”), and Google Play’s User Data policy.

Short version. Vibematcher is for adults (18+) at the same live event. We collect only what matching needs: your first name, age, gender, who you want to match with, one photo, three vibe tags, an optional Instagram handle, and what you do in the app (check-ins, “I felt this” taps, likes, matches, chats, reports). We check your precise location once, when you tap check-in, and do not store it. There is no email or phone login: a random ID on your phone is your account. We do not sell your data, show ads or use analytics or tracking SDKs. You can delete everything from Profile → Delete my account & data.

3. Legal basis: your consent

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent (DPDP Act s.6), which you give by ticking “I agree to the Terms, Privacy Policy and Community Guidelines” during onboarding, after seeing an itemised notice of what we collect. Location is used only after a separate in-app notice and your device’s permission prompt. Some processing is required by law even without consent, such as keeping safety records (section 6) and answering lawful orders (s.7 DPDP Act).

Withdrawing consent. You can withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, by deleting your account (Profile → Delete my account & data) or by writing to the Grievance Officer. Withdrawal does not affect processing already done. After withdrawal we stop processing and erase your data, except what the law requires us to keep (section 6).

5. How we protect your data

  • All traffic uses HTTPS (encrypted in transit).
  • Every database table is locked with row-level security; the app can only call specific server functions, and each call must present your device secret, which we store only as a hash.
  • Location is never stored; distance and location are never returned to other users.
  • Access to moderation tools is limited to the founder’s admin device.
  • Reports immediately hide the reported profile until reviewed.
  • We will keep access logs and backups as the DPDP Rules require (r.6) and review this setup as we grow.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach happens, we will inform affected users without delay through an in-app notice (we have no email or phone number for you), describing what happened, likely consequences, what we are doing and what you can do, and we will report to the Data Protection Board of India as required (DPDP Rules r.7, including a detailed report within 72 hours), and to CERT-In where the law requires.

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • Access a summary of your personal data and how it is processed (s.11): write to the Grievance Officer.
  • Correct or update it (s.12): Profile → Edit profile, or write to us.
  • Erase it (s.12): Profile → Delete my account & data, or the web route at delete-account.html.
  • Withdraw consent (s.6(4)), as above.
  • Grievance redressal (s.13): write to the Grievance Officer; we answer within the times on the Grievance page and never later than 90 days (DPDP Rules r.14(3)).
  • Nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to (s.14): write to us with the nominee’s name and contact.
  • Complain to the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied with our response.

To identify you we need your Vibematcher ID, shown in Profile → Legal & privacy (the first 8 characters of your device ID). Because there is no login, requests from a different device may need extra details (see the deletion page).

8. Children

Vibematcher is only for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly process personal data of children (anyone under 18) and we do not seek parental consent, because children may not use the service at all. The app blocks anyone who enters an age under 18, and our server rejects any profile under 18. If we learn that a user is under 18, we remove the profile and delete their data, keeping only what the law requires for safety. Report a suspected minor from any profile or chat (“Underage / child safety”). See our Child Safety Standards.

9. Changes to this policy

We will show the updated policy in the app and ask you to accept it again before you continue when we make a material change. The version date at the top always shows the current version. Under the IT Rules we also remind users of our rules periodically.
